Closed states at a glance (the black between the wings is the hardware notch)

Key choices

  • The notch is black in both appearances, because it extends the hardware. Idle draws nothing beside it. A running session adds two short wings: marks and a breathing dot on the left, the elapsed time on the right.
  • Hover opens it to 540 pt with three pages: Now, Usage, Shelf. The tabs sit on either side of the hardware notch, so nothing hides behind it.
  • The Now card has room: 56 pt rows, the project beside the provider, the task below and the time on the right. An agent group shows its members as chips. The notch lists three sessions at most and sends the rest to the panel.
  • An ask opens the notch by itself, the only thing that does. It takes ↩ to allow and ⌘⌫ to deny, and Esc closes it without answering. Try them on the Ask state.
  • A finished turn widens the wings for 2.5 s with a green check and the time it took, then closes. It never opens the notch.
  • Shelf controls are 28 to 30 pt pills, files are 44 pt icons with a 22 pt remove button on hover, and the whole page is a drop target. Try dropping a real file on it.
  • Motion: the shape morphs over 250 ms with a slight overshoot. The new content is in place on the first frame and only fades over 120 ms.

Audit findings fixed

  • Now card cramped → 56 pt rows, 540 pt wide, three rows at most
  • Tiny shelf controls → 28–30 pt pills and a 22 pt remove button
  • "Working" repeated → a breathing dot and a time, no word
  • Usage in a new style → the same two columns and gauge as the panel

Open questions

  • Should the notch open on hover (as shown, with a 150 ms hover delay) or only on a click?
  • Should the finished notice show on every turn, or only on turns longer than a set time (2 min?)
  • On a Mac without a notch, should the same shape hang from the menu bar, or should the notch features be off?
  • Should the shelf keep files after a restart, or clear at quit?
  • With two asks waiting, should ↩ answer the first and move to the next, or close after one?
