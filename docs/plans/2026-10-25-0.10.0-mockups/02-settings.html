Proposed page list

  1. General: login, menu bar icon, panel, updates, monitor
  2. Providers: the one list (Track, Menu bar, Panel, order), each with its own sub-page
  3. Alerts: banners, sounds and Focus, arranged by event
  4. Shortcuts
  5. Devices: hardware only
  6. Notch & Shelf
  7. Utilities & Toys
  8. Remote: Stream Deck and phone
  9. Privacy: every request that leaves the Mac, in one place, all off until turned on
  10. Advanced: links, diagnostics, import and export
  11. Sidebar footer: Studio ↗. Every light setting lives there.

Key choices

  • This uses the macOS 26 System Settings pattern: a floating glass sidebar, a toolbar title with back and forward buttons, grouped inset forms and sub-pages (try Providers › Claude). Folds inside folds are gone.
  • A selected sidebar row is an accent fill with white text and a white-rimmed icon. It reads clearly in both appearances.
  • Providers is a single table with logos and Track, Menu bar and Panel columns. Drag a row to reorder. Turning off Track greys out the other two columns, because nothing is collected. Providers that are not installed stay in their own group at the bottom.
  • Devices shows one card per device. A missing device collapses to one line. A stopped monitor shows one banner at the top, not a note on every row (try the toggle above).
  • Every utility row has one switch, and clicking the row opens its options. Toys get their own section with live illustrations.

Audit findings fixed

  • Sidebar selection unreadable in dark mode
  • Providers chosen in three places with checkboxes and arrows
  • Devices about 2,600 px tall with folds inside folds. It now fits on one screen.
  • "Monitor not connected" repeated on every row
  • Utility rows with two controls
  • Blend mode set in three places. It now lives only in Studio › Mix.
