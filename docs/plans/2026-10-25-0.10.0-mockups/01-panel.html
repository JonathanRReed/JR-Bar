Key choices

  • Three sections, each said once: Needs you, Agents, Usage. A working state is a breathing dot and an elapsed time, never the word. Only loud states (asking, failed) and Done get a word, so "Working" no longer appears three times.
  • An agent group is one row in the root agent's colour: "Claude · 3 workers". The disclosure shows the members with a tree line. A cross-provider group shows the member marks stacked. An unlinked worker is dim and says it gets no share of the strip.
  • Usage shows 5 h and 7 d as two columns. Each has its own number and a thin continuous bar in the provider's colour, the same colour as its bar in the menu bar icon.
  • Stale numbers turn grey and show their age under the number. Orange means an ask and nothing else. Red appears only as the small icon on a failing source.
  • A window that reset shows Reset in indigo with its time, never the old percentage.
  • Edit happens in place. Drag handles appear, the numbers give way to two eye toggles (Menu bar, Panel), and the menu bar icon updates live. Track stays in Settings, because turning it off stops collection.
  • The footer has a Studio button with a live 8-LED mirror of what the strip is playing, plus ⌥⌘L.
  • Motion: the panel fades in and rises 4 pt over 140 ms. The rows are already in place on the first frame.

Audit findings fixed

  • One % for two windows → 5 h and 7 d each have a number
  • "Working" three times → said once, in the header count
  • Stale looked like a warning → grey with its age
  • Providers chosen in three places → one list, edited here or in Settings
  • Studio buried → a footer button and a shortcut

Open questions

  • Should a usage cell show used or left? The mockup shows used, which matches how the menu bar bars fill.
  • Should the reset times sit in the row subtitle (as shown) or appear only on hover?
  • When nothing needs you, should the Usage section move to the top?
  • Should a done session leave the list after a set time (5 min?) or stay until the next turn?
  • Should Hide on "not found" also turn off Track, or only hide the row?
