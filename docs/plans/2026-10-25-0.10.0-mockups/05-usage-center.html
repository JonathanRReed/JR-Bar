Key choices

  • A sidebar of providers and one detail page, instead of one endless column. Overview is a table: one row per provider, the same two quota columns as the panel, then today's tokens and cost.
  • Four things, four units, four places. Quota is a percent of a limit with its reset. Tokens are counts on a chart. Context is tokens out of a window, per open session. Cost is dollars, marked as an estimate.
  • Every quota lane has its own number: 5 h and 7 d never share one percent. One gauge style everywhere (a continuous capsule with a pace tick); no rings, no heatmap, no block meters.
  • The token chart: smooth curves that never dip below zero, faint overlapping fills, y-axis steps of 1, 2 or 5, a crosshair with a tooltip that stays inside the chart at both edges. Click a total to hide its series.
  • States keep content in place. Refreshing spins the button and leaves the numbers. Scanning draws what it has read so far. Stale greys the number and gives its age. Empty and "no quota source" say so in words.

Audit findings fixed

  • Endless column mixing rings, bars and a heatmap → sidebar + a fixed page of four sections
  • One % for 5 h and 7 d → one number per lane
  • Stale shown as a warning → grey number and age, plus a Retry
  • Tiny fonts and one-off colours → the shared ramp (11 pt minimum) and the token sheet's colours

Open questions

  • Should Usage Center open on Overview, or on the provider you last looked at?
  • Should cost appear at all? It is an estimate from list prices and will be wrong on a subscription plan. It could hide behind a setting.
  • Cache reads dwarf everything else on the chart. Should Cache start hidden (as shown) or visible?
  • The old heatmap is gone. Is the 30-day chart enough, or should a calendar view come back as a separate range?
  • Should the context section show only open sessions, or the last few closed ones too?
