# Systems audit — 2026-09-16

Owner's ask: "audit all of our systems, look at the best open-source
and paid versions of everything we're making native versions of,
compare ourselves against them, and see what we're doing right and
wrong." Three research passes (menu bar managers; notch and Dock
apps; Fold, LED strips and agent HUDs) plus the two code reviews from
the night before. Sources are linked inline; measurements are ours
where marked.

## The correction that reframes the menu bar

This Mac runs **macOS 27.0 (26A428)**, not 26 — `sw_vers`, checked
today. Every "macOS 26" in the repo, the ledger and the memory notes
describes this machine, and the behaviours we measured (one composited
menu bar drawn by `MenuBarAgent`, no per-item windows, the `«`
overflow button, over-long items ejected rather than pushing) are
**macOS 27 "Golden Gate"** behaviours
([Badgeify](https://badgeify.app/macos-27-golden-gate-menu-bar-changes/),
[Ice PR #995](https://github.com/jordanbaird/Ice/pull/995)). Tahoe 26
has no `«` at all; items that do not fit are silently dropped.

## Menu Bar

**What the field does on 27.** Every spacer-based manager broke: Ice
(maintainer silent), Hidden Bar, Dozer, Vanilla, Barbee, SaneBar
(went free because "27 breaks every manager"). The ones that work —
Bartender 7, Thaw, Pelmet, Ice's pending #995 — all use the private
**assessment-mode assertion** in `MenuBarClientCore`
(`MBAssessmentModeConfiguration` / `MBAssessmentModeAssertion`): an
allowlist of system items and bundle identifiers; everything else is
concealed by `MenuBarAgent` itself. No spacer, no blank, no `«`. The
API is dlopen'd and resolved at runtime; a CLI can call it but only a
signed app bundle's allowlist is honoured (we confirmed both halves
today: our probe activates "ok" from the shell and hides nothing).

Known costs, from Ice #995 and Pelmet's notes, that any user of it
must handle:

- `MenuBarAgent` ignores clicks on its own items (clock, battery,
  Wi-Fi) while an assertion is live. Managers hold the click back
  with an event tap, lift concealment, replay the click, re-acquire.
  Control Center opens from an AX press with items still concealed.
- Concealed items drop out of AX (Pelmet) or report stale frames
  (Ice): what is hidden must be tracked per process, never inferred
  from a frame.
- `MenuBarAgent` reorders items on its own, so bar order no longer
  says which section an item is in; sections are kept per app.
- Activate the new assertion before invalidating the old one, so an
  app concealed on both sides of a change never flickers.
- Granularity is per bundle identifier (an app with two items loses
  both); a bundle-less helper process can never be hidden.

**What we do.** The spacer model (JR-Bar's icon grows a blank spacer
that packs items left of it into Apple's overflow) is the best a
public-API design can do on 27 and it is exactly what the owner is
looking at: a `«`, ~200 pt of blank, then the icon. It also chases
every bar shift (the recording indicator, the clock widening), and
the fit edge is a guess we keep learning. The reveal gestures, the
Item Bar, the hidden-items submenu and the card are all fine and
reusable.

**Verdict: wrong mechanism for this OS.** Replace the hider's core
with a **concealer** on the assessment assertion, keeping the spacer
engine only as the fallback where the framework does not resolve.
Sections become an explicit per-app map (shown / hidden / always
hidden) edited in the card, seeded once from what the spacer hides
today; reveal = invalidate; rehide = re-activate; a click on a
system item lifts and replays. Bartender/Pelmet-class extras worth
borrowing later: a keyboard search that clicks an item, per-display
and per-Focus profiles (our trigger rules already exist), and a
"withdraw the boundary while idle" rule if we ever need the spacer.

## Notch

**Field.** Alcove is the consensus best feel: nothing at rest, ears
appear only for a live activity, hover is opt-in (a tell — pulse and
shadow — not an open), click/swipe opens, expand and collapse are
separately tuned springs with deliberate overshoot, swipes are
rate-limited, fullscreen hides. Boring Notch publishes its numbers:
hover open 0.3 s floor, open spring response 0.42 / damping 0.8,
close 0.45 / 1.0, hover-out closes after 100 ms. NotchNook pulses on
hover and bounces on open. Every one of them paints over the menu
bar strip beside the notch whenever ears are up; none documents
click-through.

**Us.** After last night: bare housing exactly the notch under the
Screen Bar's ears, ear hover drives the island, 0.12 s hover open,
expand response 0.34 / 0.86, collapse 0.32 / 0.95, frame spring
centred on every tick, hosting view sized by the toy. Card 320 pt.
That is at or past the published numbers. Gaps: hover-open is on by
default (Alcove's default is click, hover opt-in); no "tell" on
hover when the island is bare; no haptic tick; fullscreen is not a
first-class state; the ears' content is ours (agent rings) which is
the whole point — Atoll and CodeIsland show that "agent sessions in
the notch" is now a category.

**Verdict: mechanism right, defaults and tells to tune.** Add the
hover tell (a 3 pt swell of the ears, not the card), keep hover-open
but at Boring Notch's 0.3 s floor when the pointer arrived from the
menu bar row, suppress hover-open in fullscreen.

## Dock

**Field.** DockDoor: open delay 0.2 s on first open *and* lateral
moves; panel centred on the icon's midX with a signed gap that
changes under magnification; positions from the last-known visible
Dock frame and **holds the Dock out** while the panel is up; hides
windowless apps by default; static one-shot captures cached 30 s so
the purple indicator blinks rarely; "Compact" no-image mode as the
indicator-free path; close/minimize/hide/fullscreen/new window/quit.
Their maintainer's position on the indicator: "nothing we can do"
beyond caching.

**Us.** After last night: clamp, re-anchor while hovered, level under
the Dock, mouse-moved events, seam grace, windowless suppressed, id-
matched thumbnails, tiles cached 0.25 s, content fills the glass. Gaps
against DockDoor: no thumbnail cache across opens (every hover blinks
the indicator, which also shifts the menu bar — now held, but still
visible), the Dock is not held out while the panel is up, no
Compact/no-capture mode, no fullscreen/new-window verbs.

**Verdict: mechanism right; borrow the cache and the hold.** Cache
captures per window id for 30 s; pin the Dock's autohide-delay while
a panel is up (we already own `AppleDockControl`); a "no captures"
mode that keeps icons + titles.

## Fold

**Field.** Bendy and its open clones (foldy, MacDuo, Bendable,
lid-plane) all render a ScreenCaptureKit capture warped in Metal
against a **black** void; MacDuo's eye-centred homography with a far-
edge fade to black is the reference; none uses a material or grey.
Pitfalls documented upstream: a black flash on order-in when the
overlay is opaque (BendMac #11), and a grey sheet whenever a material
or non-clear background sits behind the fold.

**Us.** The grey was our own "Frost" knob, shipped at 0.65 — the
room's void lifts to a milk. Default is 0 now (black room) and the
shipped 0.65 is migrated. The renderer already does the layered
blur, hinge vignette and dissolve to black. Lid sensor read matches
the field (HID 0x05AC/0x8104, page 0x20, usage 0x8A).

**Verdict: right; one default was wrong.** Later: MacDuo's
eye-centred homography would make the fold "hold the angle" better
than a tilt.

## Agent HUD, Screen Bar, quota

**Field.** CodexBar (69 providers, meter icon, reset countdowns,
incident badges), ClaudeBar (colour bands, notch Live Activity),
AgentBar/agent-monitor (hooks only, five notification moments, keep-
awake, quiet hours), AgentChirp (needs-input ordering: longest waiting
first; three amber flashes; green for 10 s on done), CodeIsland and
Vibe Island (approve/deny and multiple-choice answers from the notch,
pane-precise jump, smart-suppress when the pane is frontmost).

**Us.** Hooks-first monitor, keep-awake (now display too), asks with
answers in the panel and card, quota rings in the ears with the
headline window, a daemon protocol. Gaps: no needs-input ordering
with beacons on the bar, no smart-suppress, no incident badge, no
countdown-to-reset on the ring.

**Verdict: right shape; the borrowable ideas are ordering, beacons,
and reset countdowns.**

## Tahoe/Golden Gate bar aesthetics

Template SF Symbol at ~16 px in the 22 pt slot, no text unless it is
a number, no material behind status content, throttle status-item
updates (frequent updates can spin `MenuBarAgent`). Our session strip
is a composite image drawn with the label colour and updates on
session changes only — fine. The `‹` hint is gone; the `«` will go
with the concealer.
