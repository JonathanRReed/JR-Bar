# Attribution and project origins

JR-Bar began as a fork of [SidePulse](https://github.com/inteliwear/sidepulse),
created by Peter Kuhar. That project provided the original foundation.
JR-Bar is now independently maintained by Jonathan R. Reed and follows its
own product direction and release process. It is no longer connected to the
upstream GitHub fork network or intended to be merged back upstream.

Repository independence does not remove the upstream project's credit or
license. The original `Copyright (c) 2026 Peter Kuhar` notice and MIT license
text are preserved in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Git history retains the authorship
of individual contributions. This document does not replace those notices,
relicense upstream code, or imply endorsement by the original project.

Other projects studied during development are credited in
[Prior art](docs/PRIOR-ART.md). Third-party components remain subject to their
respective licenses and notices.

SidePulse is a trademark of InteliWEAR LLC, which publishes SidePulse Pro and
SidePulse Dot. JR-Bar is based on SidePulse by Peter Kuhar and InteliWEAR LLC.
It is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or
endorsed by InteliWEAR LLC.

JR-Bar does not use the SidePulse name, logo or app icon as its own branding.
Its name, app icon and menu bar glyph are its own, drawn in code
(`app/scripts/make-icon.swift`, `StatusIconRenderer`). The background program
that keeps a card in the SD slot from being ejected is called JR-Bar SD Eject
Guard; an older install that carried a SidePulse-era name is replaced by it
on the next install. Where JR-Bar mentions SidePulse, it names the hardware it
works with (for example "SidePulse Pro" and "SidePulse Dot", and the volume
names those devices report), which is nominative use. The two product photos in
`media/` show that hardware and came from the upstream project. The upstream
trademark policy is in the
[sidepulse-ios repository](https://github.com/inteliwear/sidepulse-ios/blob/5eec8b41377925d95a47845ea265d8c2b223f848/TRADEMARKS.md).
